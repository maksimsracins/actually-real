# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday people who've seen a photo online (or been sent one) and want a quick, credible read on whether it's AI-generated — not forensic professionals, but skeptical, curious users who want to feel like they got a real answer, not a toy. They use it in the moment of doubt: right after seeing a suspicious photo, often on their phone, often about to decide whether to trust or share it.

## Product Purpose

Scan a photo and return an evidence-backed verdict (Likely Real / Likely AI / Uncertain) built from four independent signals — a trained AI-detection model, compression-artifact analysis (ELA), camera metadata, and Content Credentials (C2PA) — with a heatmap showing exactly which regions triggered the result and plain-English explanation cards per signal. Success is the user trusting the verdict enough to act on it, and returning (or upgrading to Unlimited) because the app earned that trust rather than asserting it.

## Brand Personality

Sleek & premium — the confidence of a premium camera or photo app, applied to a forensic tool. Precise, calm, unhurried. It never oversells certainty: when the app is uncertain, that uncertainty should look intentional and considered, not like a broken or unfinished state.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS/AI-tool dashboard: cream/beige backgrounds, soft pastel rounded cards, gradient-clip-text headlines, Inter-everywhere typography, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section. Also avoid anything childish or gimmicky (cartoon icons, bouncy playful motion) — this undermines trust in a tool whose entire value is credibility.

## Design Principles

- **Evidence over opinion.** Every visual choice should reinforce that the verdict is backed by inspectable signals (heatmap, per-signal breakdown), never presented as a black-box score.
- **Confidence without overclaiming.** Premium, bold visual treatment — but never implies more certainty than the underlying data supports. "Uncertain" is a designed state, not an apology.
- **One primary action per screen.** The scan flow (capture/pick → analyze → verdict) stays uncluttered; nothing competes visually with the current step.
- **Premium restraint.** Polish comes from typography, spacing, and material depth (shadow, subtle gradient, precise motion) used deliberately — not decoration for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA. Verdict states (Likely Real / Likely AI / Uncertain) must be distinguishable by icon/shape/label in addition to color, since color alone isn't reliable for a decision-bearing result (colorblind-safe by construction, not just by contrast ratio).
